Your first session

Use this order for a typical working session.

01

Sign in securely

Enter your assigned username and password, then complete the one-time code step when prompted. Use Account security to verify the email address used for recovery.

Tip: never share an OTP and always sign out on a shared computer.
02

Find or add records

Open Students to search by name or institutional ID. Add a record only when your role permits it, and check the required fields before saving.

Keep records within the institution scope shown by your account.
03

Review before committing

For CSV imports, upload the file, inspect valid and rejected rows, read warnings, and confirm only when the preview is understood. A preview does not write records.

Use synthetic or appropriately authorized test data in this prototype.
04

Resolve exceptions

Use the review queue for duplicate candidates, quality findings and cases that need a decision. Similarity scores and automated rules are signals—not proof of identity.

Record a clear reason for important review decisions.
05

Check the audit trail and sign out

Use Audit history to review available activity records, then log out when the session is complete.

Displayed timestamps use India Standard Time (IST).
ACCESS & RESPONSIBILITY

Who should do what?

Use the least-privilege role that fits the work. The application enforces permissions on server routes.

A

Institution administrator

Manages users and institution settings, assigns appropriate access, and deactivates accounts when access is no longer needed.

O

Operator

Maintains student records, prepares imports and completes operational data-entry tasks within permitted areas.

V

Verifier

Reviews exceptions, checks supporting information and records decisions. A local check does not establish an official outcome.

R

Auditor

Reviews available records and audit history with limited routine editing access, supporting traceability and oversight.

Data and verification limits

What the status labels mean—and what they do not mean.

  • Use synthetic or authorized test data in this prototype.
  • CSV warnings and local rules help find issues; they do not validate identity against an official source.
  • Source comparisons use institution-provided data and should be reviewed by a person.
  • This app does not connect to APAAR, ABC or DigiLocker unless a separate authorized integration is implemented and configured.
  • Do not enter real student identifiers in public demo environments.

Account recovery

Steps to take if you cannot sign in.

  1. Choose Forgot password? on the sign-in page.
  2. Use the verified email address linked to your account.
  3. Use reset links before they expire; the current flow sets a limited validity window.
  4. If email delivery fails, contact your institution administrator.

The deployment should use configured SMTP. Never expose authentication codes or credentials in public logs.

Administrator safety checklist

Before using the app beyond a local demo.

  • Set a strong private SECRET_KEY and admin password.
  • Configure SMTP using environment variables.
  • Use HTTPS and enable secure session cookies in production.
  • Back up the database before upgrades and test recovery from a backup.
  • Never commit .env, credentials, or real student data to GitHub.

Need help?

Use the information on the page where the issue occurs.

For a data issue, note the page, record reference and validation message. For an access issue, contact the institution administrator. Do not send passwords, OTPs or secret tokens when reporting a problem.
